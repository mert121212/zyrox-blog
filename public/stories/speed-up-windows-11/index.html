PC Performance

Speed Up Windows 11 in 30 Mins

No shady registry cleaners. Clean, built-in tweaks that actually make your PC feel brand new.
Step 1

Purge Startup Apps

Ctrl + Shift + Esc → Startup Apps

Disable Discord, Spotify, Steam, and Epic Games on boot. They keep background helpers running 24/7 that eat RAM and CPU cycles.

This alone shaves 10–20 seconds off your boot time.
Step 2

Disable Transparency Effects

Settings → Accessibility → Visual Effects

Turn off Transparency effects and Animation effects. Windows 11 rendering lag drops instantly on low & mid-tier GPUs.
