Black Screen? Don't Panic!

9 out of 10 boot failures aren't dead hardware. Check these 3 things before spending any money.
#1 Cause

Reseat Your RAM Sticks

Slots 2 and 4 (A2/B2)

Unclip both RAM sticks, blow out the slots, and press firmly until both ends click. Test booting with only one stick in slot 2.

Over 50% of black-screen no-POST errors are loose memory channels.
#2 Cause

GPU Port & Clear CMOS

DisplayPort in the GPU, not Motherboard!

Make sure your monitor is plugged into the graphics card. Then remove the silver CR2032 coin battery for 5 minutes to reset BIOS.
